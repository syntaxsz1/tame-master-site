What she does

Every feature can be switched on or off per server, and every piece of wording — the welcome, the rules, the guide — can be edited by the server's owners. Nothing is locked.

Welcome & guided setup

New members get a warm welcome card and their starter role. Owners tap one button and Ember walks the whole setup, matching or creating the channels and roles she needs.

Stars & ranks

Chatting and hanging out in voice earns stars. Stars climb a rank ladder the owners design themselves — 2 to 10 ranks, their own names, goals, and roles, granted automatically.

Music

Play songs and build a queue from buttons on a tidy now-playing card. Search-pick, vote-skip, fair queue tools, and one-tap cozy stations for quiet nights.

Tickets & applications

Members open private ticket channels that staff can claim and close. Staff applications arrive with the answers attached and Accept / Decline buttons for the team.

Role panels & panel maker

Tap-to-pick role panels for vibes and favourite spots — plus a panel maker, so owners can build their own role and link button panels in a few taps.

Polls & giveaways

Button-vote polls with live counts that survive restarts, and tap-to-enter giveaways with a fair draw, winner pings, and staff rerolls.

Star shop

Members spend the stars they earn on profile-card scenery. Owners run their own shop: they set the prices and switch items on or off for their server.

Logs that explain themselves

One plain sentence per event — who joined, who left, who was warned and why — in a staff channel the owners choose. Deeper logs (edits, deletes, voice moves) exist too, off unless the owners turn them on.

Birthdays & daily questions

Birthday shout-outs on the morning of the day, a fresh question every evening to get people talking, and a Star of the Week celebration every Monday.

Set up in three clicks

No dashboard, no documentation to read, no settings file. If a server can tap buttons, it can run Ember.

1

Add Ember

Use the invite button and pick your server. Ember arrives with a short, exact list of permissions — no administrator access.

2

Tap “Set me up”

Her first message in your server carries a button only admins can use. One tap opens the setup wizard.

3

Let her do the work

“Set everything up automatically” matches what your server already has, creates what is missing — staff channels are born private — and posts the welcome panels. Done.

And the small things

Profile cards & badges

Every member gets a drawn profile card — avatar, rank, star progress, badges — and a choice of scenery for it, some earned through the star shop.

Around the fire

One shared fire per server that the whole community keeps fed, notes in bottles delivered in the evening, hugs, hot cocoa, and stargazing for the quiet hours.

Nights together

Karaoke hosting with a fair queue, movie nights with votes and star ratings, a looking-for-group board, event reminders, and join-to-create voice rooms.
