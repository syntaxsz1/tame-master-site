Game Guide

Every move, ability and element in the game — the same numbers the battle engine uses. Moves unlock as your monster levels: 2 basics from catch, a Strong move at Level 10, and a signature or Apex move at Level 25. A move's power also scales a little with the monster's rarity (Common ×0.90 up to Divine ×1.22).

Elements

Six elements in one ring. Hitting the element yours beats deals ×1.25 damage; hitting the element that beats yours deals ×0.8. Everything else is neutral.

Flame → Leaf → Tide → Volt → Frost → Shade → back to Flame — each element beats the next one.

The Lab — Crafting

At Level 25 a monster can be crafted in the Lab (the Craft button on its page, or in Arena Prep). Crafting feeds other monsters to the crafter — fed monsters are gone forever — and what it gets is permanent: it can never be removed or replaced. Crafted monsters carry the flask mark and can be sold on the marketplace with their crafts. A monster's float never changes.

CraftCostWhat it does
Graft a MoveFeed 1 monster Steal one element-kit move the fed monster knows — any element. Signatures and Divine moves can never be grafted.
Swap AbilityFeed 1 monster Take the fed monster's born ability as this monster's ability.
Special MoveFeed 3 monsters Pick one of the 4 Lab specials below.
Swift AbilityFeed 3 monsters Gain Swift: +10% Speed in battle. No monster is born with it.
