How Battles Work

You fight with 1 monster out at a time. Your team waits on the bench — you can switch any turn.

Each turn, you pick 1 move. The faster monster moves first.

Every move has an element. Hit an enemy with the element it is weak to, and you deal extra damage.

Moves unlock as your monster levels up: 2 basic moves from the start, a stronger move at Level 10, and its best move at Level 25.

There are 6 elements. Each one is strong against one element, and weak against one other. A strong hit deals ×1.25 damage. A weak hit deals only ×0.8. All other matchups deal normal damage. Follow the arrows: each element beats the next one in the ring.

Some moves raise or lower stats during a fight — Attack, Speed or Accuracy, one step at a time. Steps reset when the monster switches out.

Every monster learns the moves of its own element. The same move hits a little harder when a rarer monster uses it (Common ×0.90, up to Mythic ×1.16).

When a monster reaches Level 25, it can be crafted in the Lab. Open the monster's page and press Craft — the button is also in Arena Prep.

Crafting feeds other monsters to yours. Fed monsters are gone forever. What your monster gets is permanent — it can never be removed or replaced. So choose well.

A crafted monster gets a small flask mark. It keeps its crafts when it is sold or traded — crafted monsters are worth more on the marketplace. Crafting never changes a monster's float.
