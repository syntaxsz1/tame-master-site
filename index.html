How it plays

Start with /start, hunt with /hunt, catch with /tame. Everything runs from the /menu button hub — tapping works as well as typing.

Float Grades

Every catch rolls a float from 0.0000000000 to 1.0. Low-float copies are graded Perfect or Pristine and are worth far more when sold or traded.

Coin Plots

Place monsters on your plot and they print coins for you, even while you are away. A plot stores up to 8 hours of coins before it fills.

Global Arena

A ranked 3v3 ladder shared by every server. Monsters have real stats, one of 12 abilities, and an element — Flame beats Leaf, Leaf beats Tide, and so on.

Marketplace

Buy and sell monsters with other players at fixed prices. Prices are listed cheapest first, and every sale is instant.

Clans & Wars

Create or join a clan, level it up, and fight 5v5 clan wars for a coin pot. Clan members earn bonus plot income, and the top clan each week pays every member 5,000 coins.

Casino

Slots, blackjack, lottery, and coinflip — all played with the coins your monsters earn.

Shinies

1 in 64 catches is a shiny. Shiny monsters sell for 5x the normal price.

Seasonal Events

Limited-time monsters spawn during events. The Halloween event is live until November 6, 2026 — its 5 monsters can never be caught again after it ends.
